Additional source-field controls

Scope
This fixed post hoc extension uses the existing 1062 HCP-83 and 695 OASIS
participants, all five participant splits, three protected masks, and the
saved random source rankings at 8, 16, and 32 sources. No additional exclusions
or source-policy optimization are performed. Both cohorts informed earlier
development; these experiments are exploratory, not independent confirmation.
There are 1260 fitted configurations: 2 cohorts x 5 splits x 3 masks x 3 budgets
x (9 known-label representations + 5 unseen-label representations).

Known-label representations
Full QSF uses six times [0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8], with interleaved real,
imaginary, and probability channels (18k dynamic features). Raw and full-row
normalized heat use 18 geomspace(0.25, 8) times. Additional QSF dynamic blocks
are probability at six times (6k), real/imaginary at six times (12k), probability
at 18 geomspace times (18k), real/imaginary at nine geomspace times (18k), and
no dynamic block (moments only). All QSF ablations retain the original six-time
mean probability response for coordinate moments and bound centers. These are
dynamic-channel ablations, not removal of probability from the whole pipeline.
All representations add six source-coordinate moments, except the moments-only
control whose total dimension is six. Equal dimension does not equate capacity
or information content. Probability responses may retain interference effects.

Shortest path
Use the original all_pairs_shortest_path helper on zero-diagonal log1p weights,
with present-edge lengths 1/(W+1e-9). Fill unreachable distances with 1.25 times
the maximum finite distance within the subject. A fully edgeless graph would
have zero filled distances; count rather than silently exclude such cases.
Scale the selected source columns by their median across all vertices and
sources, floored at 1e-6. Concatenate scaled distances and exp(-scaled distance)
(2k dynamic features). Use the exponential response for six coordinate moments
and source-frame centers. This baseline contains no QSF features. Its finite
surrogates for disconnected pairs are conventional features, not physical
propagation across components.

Unseen-label supervision
Withhold each protected mask's coordinates in every participant before building
learning inputs. Sources are outside this mask. Train and validate on every
remaining label except sources; nonfinite target coordinates contribute no row.
Full graphs and atlas correspondence remain available. Test on the original
held-out participants and protected labels. The five representations are full
QSF, raw heat, normalized heat, shortest path, and fitting-only template QSF.
No coordinate atlas or hidden-label coordinate prior is admissible. Templates
average fitting-participant weights only, with each person's own source geometry.
Validation extent uses only permitted coordinates, floored at one. Full-subject
extent is attached after predictions are fixed for test scoring only. This is
unseen coordinate supervision at known vertices, not a new atlas or new vertices.
Differences from known-label results also reflect changed supervision and
validation normalization; they are not pure estimates of memorization.

Decoder and numerical recovery
Reuse the primary tanh3 preprocessing, positive summed-loss ridge grid,
one-standard-error rule, radii [1,2,4], source-only fallbacks, and validation
choice between projection-only and bounded residual. Refit on fitting plus
validation participants. Calibration remains unused. No test-based choices.
If direct SVD does not converge, retry the same full selection routine with QR
and augmented least squares: solve [R; sqrt(alpha) I] B = [Q'Y_centered; 0].
No features, penalties, or observations are removed. Log the recovery and
compare the selected refit to direct augmented least squares on the full design.
The original primary benchmark retains its original default solver.

Reporting and checks
Save all grids, selections, coefficients, predictions, and participant metrics
under the local work directory; ship only aggregate outputs. Average repeated
records within each participant before group means. Pair on split, mask, and
participant, using 5000 paired participant-bootstrap resamples. The seed is the
first eight SHA-256 hexadecimal digits of the contrast's stable name. Intervals
are conditional, not family-aware or multiplicity-corrected. Overlapping
known-label heat contrasts retain the original main-paper intervals; the
additional-control export uses its own fixed resampling seeds and can differ
slightly at interval endpoints despite identical predictions.
Verify source/target separation, all selections, and all prediction-derived
metrics. At split 1/mask 1/32 sources for every representation and cohort,
check the solver independently and poison hidden coordinates for unseen-label
models. Repeat both checks for any recovered unseen-label fit. Refuse incomplete
reporting. Native-coordinate RMSE is not certified as millimetres.
